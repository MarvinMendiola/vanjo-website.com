About Blue Dragon One

Blue Dragon One Trading Corporation, formerly Bike Rides2Fortune Corporation, was registered in September 2016 and is supported by the Intramuros Holdings Group. We help retailers, service shops, and businesses source products, coordinate deliveries, and get after-sales support.

Our mission

Provide dependable products and technology solutions that help customers operate, grow, and adapt.

Our vision

Become a trusted Philippine trading and solutions company known for quality, practical technology, and reliable execution.
